Notes are meant to be quick and unfinished.[^1] They can still have headings, links and figures, and every note gets the same contents rail and comments as an essay.

## Why notes are different

An essay has an argument and a point of view. A note is closer to a lab notebook entry: a thing you noticed, a half-formed idea, a link worth keeping.[^2]
